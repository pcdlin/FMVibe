Amp: UI drafts

Two layouts for the Amp effect's card, which shows in the effects row and in the effects graph's inspector, and the pieces around it. The recommended one is A: a wide card (1.75 places, 312 px; the Param EQ and 3-Band Dynamics take 1.5) that shows every control, the amp on the left and the cabinet on the right, in the order the sound goes through them. At 1.5 places the amp's three knob columns are 41 px apart and their labels run into each other.

A: wide card, amp | cab side by side (recommended)

B: a card of the usual width, with AMP | CAB tabs

C: around the card
